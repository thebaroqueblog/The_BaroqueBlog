The Ficker family of violin makers

The Fickers were a family of violin makers from Markneukirchen in the Vogtland. Two lines can be traced: Line I goes back to Johann Caspar Ficker (1697–1772), Line II to Johann Heinrich Ficker (1708–1779). Whether both lines descend from a common ancestor, presumably Johann Christian Ficker I, has not been proven. The Fickers built violins over several generations, from the 18th century well into the 19th. The youngest maker listed here, Christian Heinrich Ficker, died in 1881.

Life dates according to Bernhard Zöbisch, Vogtländischer Geigenbau bis 1850.
