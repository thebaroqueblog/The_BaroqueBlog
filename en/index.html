A family of violin makers from Markneukirchen and the traces they left behind.

A research project by Domenic Rutschmann

Violin, Johann Christian Ficker I, Markneukirchen, c. 1705

Over six generations, the Fickers built violins in Markneukirchen. I collect their surviving instruments, compare them and try to find out how the hand of each individual maker can be recognised.
